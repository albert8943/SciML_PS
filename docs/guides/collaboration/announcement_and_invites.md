## A. LinkedIn / website news announcement

**Suggested title:** Open teaching notes: SciML for Power Systems (public preview)

**Body (edit freely):**

I am opening a living teaching book on scientific machine learning for power systems — short form **SciML for PS** — as a public preview (v0.x, under active development).

Focus: **dynamics**, **stability assessment**, and **system identification**, with shared foundations, classical baselines beside SciML methods, and labs beside the notes.

To our knowledge, there is not yet an established open, collaborative living textbook that organizes scientific machine learning around power-system dynamics, stability assessment, and system identification as three engineering applications. Related research codes, short courses, and general SciML notes exist; this resource aims to fill that teaching gap.

- Notes: https://sciml4ps.albertpoulose.com  
- Contribute: https://sciml4ps.albertpoulose.com/contribute  
- Source: https://github.com/albert8943/SciML_PS  

Content is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; teaching code is MIT. Feedback and collaborators (including future section editors for Dynamics, Stability, Identification, and Labs) are welcome. Please treat the resource as under development until the first stable edition (v1.0).
